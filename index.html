Vanguard

A private desktop workspace for continuing AI-assisted work across accounts and company projects.

Google Slides, with access you choose

Vanguard connects directly to Google to read presentation text and structure, retrieve slide previews, and apply requested edits. Click Connect in Vanguard, sign in with Google, and choose the decks this company connection may access.

The connector requests only drive.file: access to specific files you select. It does not request access to your entire Drive. Vanguard exposes no Drive-wide search or Drive file-deletion tool.

Your accounts, separate connections

One desktop app registration supports your Google accounts. Each company connection stores its own authorization and deck selection in the Mac’s Keychain. Google handles sign-in; passwords and MFA codes are not entered in chat.

This site contains app information only. It does not host Google sign-in callbacks, authorization tokens, decks, or account data.
