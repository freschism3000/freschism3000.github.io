Google Slides connector privacy

Effective 24 November 2026. This notice describes the direct Google Slides connector in Vanguard, a private desktop application operated by its owner.

Access and purpose

The connector requests only Google’s https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file permission. You choose presentation files in Google’s picker. Vanguard reads the selected presentations’ identifiers, titles, structure, text and slide previews to perform your requested work, and sends authorized presentation edits directly to Google. It checks the current company’s selected deck list before making presentation requests. It does not request whole-Drive access or expose a Drive file-deletion operation.

Storage and security

Google authorization tokens and selected deck identifiers and titles are stored in the macOS Keychain, separately for each company connection. Temporary access tokens are cached in memory. Google account passwords and MFA codes remain in Google’s sign-in flow. Vanguard’s Google integration communicates directly with Google and does not use Pipedream.

Presentation tool results and requested edits can become part of Vanguard’s local conversation history and applicable backups or exports. Local history is retained until removed through the app’s data-management controls. Disconnecting Google does not erase existing conversation history or undo presentation edits.

Sharing and AI assistance

When you use an AI agent to work on a deck, relevant presentation content and tool results are supplied to the AI provider selected for that conversation. That provider processes them under your account’s applicable terms and data settings. You control the selected account and project approval settings. Do not connect material you are not authorized to provide to that provider.

Vanguard does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI models. Its use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your controls

Use Choose Decks in Vanguard to change the app’s permitted selection. Disconnect from Vanguard removes the locally stored Google authorization and blocks future connector calls. To revoke the Google-side authorization, use Google’s third-party connections settings. Previously shared content remains subject to the retention controls of the systems that received it.

This information site

These static pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They contain no analytics scripts, forms or Google authentication callbacks. GitHub may process normal web-request metadata under its own privacy policy. No deck or token is sent to this website.

Contact

For questions, contact the app operator through the user-support email displayed on Google’s Vanguard consent screen. Do not send passwords, tokens or presentation contents in support requests.
